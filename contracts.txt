| Order Type      | Recommended Modeling Basis          | Rationale                                    |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Market Buy**  | Execute at **Ask**                  | That’s the best price available to a buyer.  |
| **Market Sell** | Execute at **Bid**                  | That’s the best price available to a seller. |
| **Limit Buy**   | Fill if **Bid ≥ Limit**             | Your limit must be at or above bid.          |
| **Limit Sell**  | Fill if **Ask ≤ Limit**             | Your limit must be at or below ask.          |
| **Stop Orders** | Triggered by **trade** (last price) | Stops usually activate on last-trade prices. |
